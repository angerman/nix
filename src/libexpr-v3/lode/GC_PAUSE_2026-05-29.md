## 6. Counter-arguments addressed

### 6.1 "But Immix is just one more attempt away from success"

Each prior variant had similar reasoning at the time.  Same trajectory.  6 variants is a STRUCTURAL signal, not engineering bad luck.  The meta-claim "GC is the right primary lever" has never itself been falsifiable in the spiral — it always slips to "but the NEXT variant will work."  This commit forces that meta-claim to face evidence.

### 6.2 "Step 11′ + 12′ + 13′ already exist; we should at least finish Immix"

Steps 11′-13′ landed and are correct + measured.  Step 14′ honest SHIP-gate re-measurement would now follow.  Per `EXIT_GC_SPIRAL_PLAN §2` quantified yields:

| Lever | Projected yield on HNE | Effort to ship |
|---|---|---|
| Continue Immix (Steps 14′-17′) | ~355-500 MB peak | 2-3 wk |
| **Cache eviction (Phase 4b LRU)** | **500-950 MB** | 2-3 wk after 3 prereqs |

**Even on its own merits, Immix is the LOWER-yield 2-3-wk investment than cache eviction.**  Step 14′ would commit 2-3 more weeks to a path projected to ship LESS than the deprioritized alternative.

### 6.3 "But cache eviction has 3 unmeasured prereqs"

True.  EXIT_GC_SPIRAL_PLAN Day 6-7 is the AUDIT of those prereqs.  If they're each a week of work + can't ship, the plan's own Rule-0 (§3.5 escalation) routes back to Immix as Week 1's work.  The plan is internally falsifiable.

### 6.4 "What about M5 under the 4 GB watchdog?"

Per `GC_DESIGN_POST_CHENEY §7` scope-reality: NO single lever delivers the ~1.76 GB needed.  Combined approach required.  Cache eviction (500-950 MB) + per-site bundle (~257 MB on HNE) + (eventual) GC could compose.  Sequencing matters — orthogonal levers FIRST, GC LAST, because:
* Orthogonal levers have higher quantified yield
* GC's measured benefit is downstream of cache contribution to arena residency
* Combined effect of orthogonal + GC may be SUB-ADDITIVE (cache eviction frees memory that GC would have); measure post-orthogonal before committing to GC

### 6.5 "The session already invested 3 weeks; don't waste it"

Sunk-cost.  The infrastructure value WAS captured (per §2.3).  The variant-selection cost is sunk.  Continuing to optimize the wrong primary lever doesn't recover the prior cost; it adds to it.
