# v3 Critical Review (2026-05-09)

A six-agent **critical** review.  Bias: look for what is wrong or
off-track, not what is working.  Confirmation bias is the enemy.

The team gets status updates daily.  This memo identifies what the
team is *not* seeing.

Confidence tags: **C** verified, **L** likely, **S** speculative.

---

## Headline

**The "inversion is progressing" narrative is overstated, the
optimizer freeze is real and compounding, and the public-facing
documentation now contradicts the canonical perf measurements by
~140 %.**

Six agents working independently arrived at the same uncomfortable
finding from different angles:

- v3-direct **cycles on real nixpkgs**.  `(import <nixpkgs>{}).hello.name`
  fails with `OP_WITH_LOOKUP: cycle while resolving 'callPackage'`
  (per `V3_DIRECT_NIXPKGS_2026-05-09.md`).  Phase 1 is scaffolding,
  not the inversion.
- The **`hello.drvPath` regression has never been run** under any v3
  mode in the 2026-05-07/09 window.  cardano-node hasn't been
  measured in 6 days.  The "we pass parity" claim has never been
  tested against a flake build.
- USAGE.md still advertises **"v3 at parity with TW on compute-bound
  benchmarks"** with 2026-05-05 numbers showing fib32 within 1 %.
  `BENCH-2026-05-08-POST-530.md` measures fib32 at **2.31× slower**.
  The public table is dishonest by drift.
- **77 of 96 unique `NIX_V3_*` flags** in source are missing from
  USAGE.md.  CLEANUP_AUDIT_2026-05-09 said 6.  The real number is
  an order of magnitude worse.
- **15 of 17** new lode/ docs added since 2026-05-07 are not indexed
  in `lode/README.md`.  88 % indexing lag.
- The **FFI is largely a ghost structure**: 20 % implementation,
  **0 % production routings**.  Categories A/B/F obsolete under
  INVERSION; only G (`applyClosure`) survives, and `applyClosure`
  itself has zero production callers.

The inversion is the right strategy.  The implementation is
half-finished, half-measured, and increasingly unhonest about its
own state.

## 1. Strategic critique — inversion path (Agent 1)

**Biggest risk nobody is currently working on:** the same
wrong-upvalue-capture bug class that has dominated reviews for two
weeks now blocks v3-direct on every realistic nixpkgs workload.
The strategic invariant in `V3_NATIVE_CONSTRAINT_2026-05-09.md`
explicitly forbids the only escape-valve TW-routing.  So the
inversion is now structurally pinned on solving the bug v3 has
never solved.

**No one has Angle A (eval-order parity) or Angle B (mid-flight
slot observation) staffed; both are diagnostic-only.**

What Phase 1 actually proves vs claims to prove:

- `run-direct-eval-tests.sh` is **26 hand-written micro-shapes**.
  No `with`-scope tests, no overlay/extends, no `inherit (X) Y`,
  no fixed-point, no nixpkgs import, no real lib code.  (**C**)
- Every shape it tests was already passing in the standalone
  `v3-eval` binary's 142/142 lang corpus.
- The plan's stated verification criterion — "Verify on
  `(import <nixpkgs> {}).hello.name`" — is **not satisfied**:
  that workload still cycles.

The honest reading: Phase 1 demonstrates **CLI plumbing wires
correctly**.  It does not demonstrate v3-direct evaluates anything
beyond lang-corpus shapes.

**"Drop TW ASAP" thesis vs reality:** in the 24 h since
REVIEW_2026-05-08, **no production TW reliance has dropped**.
`applyClosure` still has 0 production callers.  The
`BENCH-2026-05-08-POST-530.md` "0 % FFI" finding is artefactual —
the synthetic suite was deliberately constructed to avoid primops
that bridge.

**Specific underprioritised action:** reproduce the
`all-packages.nix:140` cycle in <100 lines of Nix that doesn't
import nixpkgs.  Five synthetic reducers have failed
(`V3_DIRECT_RCA_PROGRESS` §4).  Until that minimal repro exists,
every fix attempt is guesswork against the full nixpkgs payload.

## 2. Performance honesty check (Agent 2)

**The team has stopped attacking the perf gap.**  100 % of recent
commit volume since 2026-05-07 has gone to inversion correctness
(#528–#548), STG flag flipping (#547), and nursery GC (#434).
The optimizer pipeline has produced exactly **one** new commit in
seven weeks (`d691c2d6b`, `analyseOccurrence` Phase A — **no
consumers wired**).

**The truth USAGE.md is not telling itself:**

| Source | Claim | Reality |
|---|---|---|
| USAGE.md (2026-05-05 numbers) | fib32 within 1 %; v3 at parity | **fib32 2.31× slower** per BENCH-2026-05-08-POST-530 |
| Public-facing perf table | "compute-bound parity" | 2.4× slower per dispatched op, uniformly |
| BENCH 2026-05-09 RCA | bytecode 2.5× bloated | 50 ops where ~20 suffice |

**The mechanical-wins gap.**  Computed-goto (#5, ~50–100 LOC, no
prereqs) and typed numeric opcodes (#8, ~200 LOC, no prereqs) have
been the #5 and #8 ranked items in OPTIMIZER_REPORT_2026-05-07 for
**three sprints**.  Combined estimated gain: 8–15 %.  Combined
estimated cost: 2–3 days.  They have not landed.  The team is
either capacity-constrained on inversion, or implicitly judging
that perf doesn't matter until correctness settles.

**The #498 question.**  #498 has been open since 2026-05-03.  Eight
diagnostic commits, twelve STG-N rescue commits that *bypass*
rather than root-cause.  The STG-* sequence is a workaround dressed
as a feature: it disables every mechanism #498 trips through.
**#498 has not been root-caused.  It has been managed via a
permanent flag, and that flag is now default-on.**

**Phase 2 perf is unmeasured.**  The one-shot TW→v3 bridge will
fire Bridge thunks per nested closure capture.  No benchmark
exists.  Phase 2 could regress real flake evaluation by 10–30 %
with the regression invisible to current benches.  Item B from
VM_TW_MEASUREMENT (extend bench to nixpkgs) is half a day of work
and has not landed.

**This week:** land mechanical pair (#5 + #8); update USAGE.md to
honesty; wire `analyseOccurrence` into DCE per OPT_OCCUR_PLAN
Phase B; land VM_TW_MEASUREMENT item B before Phase 2 starts.

## 3. Bug-class pattern (Agent 3)

**Verdict: converging in trajectory, whack-a-moling in tactics.**

| # | Locus | Fix kind |
|---|---|---|
| 496 | publish-walk gating | heuristic-with-cleanup |
| 497 | `isComplexFromExpr` whitelist | pure heuristic |
| 528 | `s_maxLevel` 0→4 | constant bump |
| 529 | extend `isComplexFromExpr` | pure heuristic |
| 530 | static lexical-with chain | **structural** |
| 530-followup | opt_inline rewriteVar | structural-completion |

**Five of seven are local heuristics.  #530 is the first genuinely
structural pivot.**

The unifying mechanism (refining REVIEW_2026-05-06 §S1): **four of
six share an exact mechanism** — an inner expression (`from`-expr,
OpUpdate LHS, with-resolved Var, captured-with target) is *eagerly*
lowered into bytecode that runs *before* the enclosing rec / let /
lambda binding settles, then forces or reads that binding
mid-construction.  Each fix pushes the inner-expr's evaluation past
the binding's settle point.

**Test of "shapes is the unifying fix" thesis:** shapes would have
prevented #496 indirectly (the publish-walk side-table that #496
broke would not exist with shape-stable identity).  Shapes would
**NOT have prevented #497/#528/#529** — those need #530-style
static-lexical-environment IR.  This is mild dissent from prior
reviews.

**Predicted next instances (already in progress):**
1. `OP_GET_UPVALUE: no closure context` — bridge-thunk identity
   instability per EVAL_ORDER_DIVERGENCE (**C**).
2. `with` inside let-rec inside formals body — Gap 3 in
   SLOT_AUDIT_HOOK_REENTRY (**L**).
3. A second `inherit (X) Y` heuristic widening for ExprIf branch /
   ExprList head / primop-call head (**C**).

**Specific structural close:** generalise #530's static
lexical-environment recording from `with` to the entire
inherit-from / let-rec from-expr surface.  Replace
`isComplexFromExpr`+thunkify with: at lower-time, every
`inherit (e) x y z` records a `LexicalFromRef { fromExpr, attrName }`
IR node.  ~300 LOC structural change retires ~150 LOC heuristic and
removes 4 env-var flags (`s_maxLevel`, `isComplexFromExpr`,
`s_thunkifyAll`, `s_noComplex`).  Subsumes #497, #528, #529, and
the still-open #498 always-thunkify investigation in one move.

## 5. FFI direction reality check (Agent 5)

**The FFI direction is largely a ghost structure today.**

**Implementation re-measured (2026-05-09):**
- ~38 callable entry points declared in `ffi.hh`.
- **6 implemented** in `ffi.cc` (up from 5 a week ago).
- Implementation ratio: 9 % → 20 %.  **Production routings: 0**.

`v3::readFile` / `v3::readDir` / `v3::pathExists` exist as bodies
but **zero call sites anywhere outside `ffi.cc` itself**.
`applyClosure` is reachable only from `test/evalscope-handles.cc`
— both call sites are negative-path (invalid handle, dead scope).

**Categories obsolete under INVERSION:** A (Parser), B (Symbol/Pos
table), F (Eval entry), H (EvalError), I (Settings), J (Logger),
K (PrimOp flags), L (StringContext), M (BlockingFFI).  Of 13
categories, **only G (`applyClosure`) is on the post-INVERSION
critical path**.

**EvalState dependency thesis falsified by data:**

| Symbol | 2026-05-07 | 2026-05-08 | 2026-05-09 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| `state.vm` | 110 | 110 | **110** |
| `state.nixEvalState` | 55 | 55 | **55** |
| `state.symbols` | 1 | 5 | **4** |

**Three sites moved in two days.  The load-bearing dependencies
are immovable.**  The FFI's "we'll wean v3 off EvalState" claim is
contradicted by every measurement.

**Phase 4 prerequisites:** `applyClosure` becomes load-bearing when
**one** TW callback site routes through it.  Counted ~10 callback
sites in `primops.cc` (`genericClosure.operator`, `filter`, `map`,
`foldl'`, `groupBy`, `partition`, `concatMap`, `any/all`, `filter`,
plus `builtins.path { filter }`).  Migrating one site is **~3 days**
work.  That nobody did so in the 24 h after CLEANUP_AUDIT named
exactly this is the signal: **the implementation was driven by
spec compliance, not by user-of-FFI demand.**

**Plugin ABI Option B:** zero non-`ffi.hh` references.  No real
consumer.  Speculative future work.

**Specific recommendation:** **PAUSE the FFI direction**.  Add
`STATUS: PAUSED — Phase 4 only` headers to FFI_PLAN docs.
Migrate `builtins.path { filter = ... }` through `applyClosure`
as the proof-of-life concrete production caller.  Build-flag-gate
Categories A/B/D/E/F/H/I/J/L/M + plugin ABI behind
`-Dv3-ffi-extended` (default off); ~340 LoC out of default build.
**Free capacity goes to with-scope upvalue blocker, Phase 2 flake
installables, and computed-goto (PR2 — three sprints unlanded).**

## 6. External validity (Agent 6)

**The team has not measured `nix build` / `nix-instantiate` against
any real flake-based workload.**

| Target | TW | v3-fhook | v3-direct |
|---|---|---|---|
| `(import <nixpkgs>{}).hello.name` | OK "hello" | OK (since #547 STG-default-on) | **CYCLE** at `OP_WITH_LOOKUP callPackage` |
| `attrNames pkgs` | OK | OK | **TIMEOUT** |
| `attrNames pkgs.haskellPackages` | not measured recently | not measured recently | **never measured** |
| `hello.drvPath` | not in any v3 regression | not in any v3 regression | **never measured** |
| `cardano-node.name` (flake) | last measured 2026-05-04 | last measured 2026-05-04 | **never measured** |

**`hello.drvPath` is conspicuously absent.**  The harness explicitly
stops at `.name` because `name` is a string attr that doesn't
dispatch through `derivationStrict`.  **Whether v3-direct can
produce a derivation hash at all is unknown.**

**cardano-node was last measured 2026-05-04** — six days untouched.
The canonical hard target across multiple memos is currently dark.

**`run-direct-eval-tests.sh` inventory** (verified line-by-line):

- ✓ Scalar literals, arithmetic, lists, simple attrs
- ✓ `let` (no mutual rec, no `lib.fix`)
- ✓ Lambdas (no `?` defaults, no ellipsis)
- ✓ ~6 primops, JSON output
- ✗ Imports / `import <nixpkgs>`
- ✗ Flakes
- ✗ Store ops / derivation
- ✗ Fetchers / NIX_PATH
- ✗ Recursive fix-points / overlays

**The "0 % FFI" finding's context:** the synthetic suite never
imports nixpkgs.  `lib.foldl' over 1..1000` runs over integers, not
over `lib.attrNames pkgs`.  The actual evidence is "v3 doesn't need
TW for arithmetic and pure data structures" — a much weaker claim
than the headline implies.

**Specific recommendation:** add
`nix-instantiate '<nixpkgs>' -A hello.drvPath` to
`run-direct-eval-tests.sh` as a known-fail regression target —
exits 0 on either correct hash or recognised failure mode, exits
2 on a new symptom.  Promote to positive when it works.  Stretch:
add `cardano-node.drvPath` (or flake equivalent) — the canonical
"if v3 ships, can it build a real package?" test.

## Cross-cutting themes

### A. The strategic invariants are stacking unfavourably

`V3_NATIVE_CONSTRAINT_2026-05-09.md` forbids the easy
TW-routing escape valve.  `STG_DEFAULT_ON_2026-05-09.md` flipped
the publish-walk-disabled mode default-on but doesn't help v3-direct.
The bug class is unsolved.  The optimizer is frozen.  The FFI is
ghost structure.  **Each invariant individually is defensible; in
combination, they pin the team on solving the hardest unsolved
problem (identity stability) with the fewest tools.**

### B. The narrative has decoupled from the evidence

- USAGE.md says "parity"; benches say 2.4× slower.
- Phase 1 is described as "landed"; the stated verification target
  (hello.name) cycles.
- The FFI is described as "23 % implemented" (REVIEW_2026-05-08);
  production routings are 0.
- "0 % FFI in synthetic bench" implies inversion success; actually
  shows the synthetic bench was constructed to avoid TW.

This isn't dishonesty — it's drift.  Each individual claim was
true at write-time.  The cumulative effect is: a reader of the
docs will believe v3 is closer to shipping than the code shows.

### C. Test coverage doesn't match strategic risk

The team's gold-standard regression net (cutover-parity) covers
language semantics, not real workloads.  cardano-node sat untouched
for 6 days while #528/#529/#530/#547 landed.  16 of 27 substantial
commits added no test.  The mechanical-discipline gates the team
needs (flag-doc sync, lode-index sync, test-with-PR) don't exist.

### D. The whack-a-mole signal is genuine

Six fixes in six different code regions in two weeks (#496/497/498/
528/529/530), all sharing approximately one bug class but resolved
by different mechanisms.  Sub-issues (#547 Phase 1, #548 with three
sub-issues spawning #547B/C/D-class fixes) suggest the rate is not
decelerating.  #530 is the first genuinely structural fix; if the
team follows up with the LexicalFromRef generalisation
(Agent 3 §recommendation), the trajectory may converge in ~1 sprint.

## Verdict

**The inversion strategy is correct.  The execution is half-finished
and the documentation is increasingly misleading about how
half-finished it is.**

Three reviews ago the worry was correctness parity.  Two reviews
ago it was decorative FFI.  Last review it was "default-mode perf
regressed and the fix is gated on a not-yet-root-caused bug."  This
review's worry is **the team is shipping at a rate that exceeds
its discipline**:
- 132 commits / 48 h.
- 1 USAGE.md update.
- 17 new lode docs / 2 indexed.
- 27 substantial commits / 11 with tests.
- 0 `[[deprecated]]` markers; 96 unique flags accumulating.
- v3-direct cycles on real nixpkgs and that's been true for 24 h
  with no public acknowledgement.

The codebase is moving fast.  The narrative is moving faster.  When
the narrative outpaces the evidence, the next sprint's planning
gets done from a wrong picture.

If the next sprint isn't **"land mechanical perf wins, generalise
#530 structurally, pause the FFI, restore real-workload regression,
fix the doc drift,"** the inversion will continue to ship behind
opt-in flags while the public-facing perf and capability table
diverge from reality.  At some point that gap closes loudly — by
a release blocker, a user bug report, or an embarrassing benchmark.
Better to close it quietly now.
